# Every chat answered: share our client chats fairly across my team

**Posted by Daniel Moreau, Sales Manager at Bluefin Travel** · Fixed price · Start today, one working day (8 hours)

---

Hi! We're Bluefin Travel, a travel agency with six consultants: Mira, Tom, Aziz, Jonas, Lena and
Sam. Clients write to us in the chat on our website about trips, bookings and problems, around
280 chats on a normal Monday. That chat is most of our sales, and right now it's a mess.

Today every chat lands on one shared list and whoever is free takes it. It sounded fair. It isn't.

## What goes wrong

- **The easy questions get answered and the hard ones wait.** "Is breakfast included?" is gone in
  a minute. "Our flight was cancelled and we're stuck at the airport" waited 43 minutes last
  Monday. Nobody wants that chat, so everyone waits for someone else to take it.
- **Some people do far more than others.** Jonas answers lots of chats, but almost never a
  difficult one. Sam does the difficult ones and looks slow.
- **Clients come back and get a stranger.** A couple who booked Japan with Aziz last year wrote
  again and got someone who knew nothing about them. They asked for Aziz by name.
- **Someone steps away and their clients just wait.** Aziz went to lunch with chats he'd taken
  and nobody noticed for over an hour.
- **Mornings are chaos.** Clients write from 7 in the morning. At 9 we open and there are twenty
  chats waiting. We tried handing chats out automatically in the spring and everyone got eight at
  once, so we went back to the list.
- **I can't see anything.** I don't know who is in, who is answering, or how long people wait
  until a client complains.

I've attached messages from my team and some client reviews (the `inbox/` folder) so you hear it
from them too. They don't all agree with each other, and some of it isn't really a problem.

## What a good day looks like

- Every client gets a first answer from a real person **within five minutes**, including the hard
  ones, and including at 9 in the morning.
- The work is shared fairly, the hard chats too. Nobody drowns while someone else is quiet.
- Clients who have travelled with us before get **their own consultant** when that makes sense.
- If someone steps away without saying so, their clients **don't get stuck** with them.
- A chat doesn't bounce from person to person like a ping-pong ball. If nobody can take it, I
  want to know.
- My team has a simple screen: what's mine, and what do I answer first.
- I have a screen that shows me **who is in, who answers and how fast**, who is waiting right now,
  and how the day went.

Some of these pull against each other. Aziz says his clients should always get him. I'd rather a
client gets a quick hello from Mira than waits twenty minutes for Aziz. And people go for coffee;
I don't want them punished for it. **You decide where the lines are, and tell me why in plain
words.** I'm not technical, so please don't send me a manual.

## What I'd like back

1. Something my team can use, that shares the chats out the way you think is right.
2. A screen for me.
3. A short note (`DECISIONS.md`): how things were before and after, what rules you chose and why,
   what you'd do next, and your answers to my team's complaints. Plain words please.

I don't mind what you build it with. My nephew says there's a test that replays a whole Monday
in a minute and shows how it would have gone. Please show me the before and after.

Thanks,
Daniel
